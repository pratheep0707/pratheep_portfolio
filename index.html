01 HR WORKFORCE DYNAMICS & ATTRITION COST ANALYSIS

Executive BI Dashboard & Turnover Cost Modeling

An end-to-end business intelligence project analyzing 1,470 employee records to uncover the true financial cost of employee turnover. Instead of just reporting a standard 16.12% attrition rate, I applied SHRM industry benchmarks and built a star schema data model to calculate a total replacement cost of $11.27M.

The analysis identified that 42% of exits occur within the first two years and proved that chronic overtime nearly triples the risk of attrition (30.5% vs. 10.4% baseline). I also engineered a composite flight-risk metric based on promotion delays and compensation tiers to provide HR leadership with a targeted retention watchlist.

Executive Key Insights & Findings
  • Early Flight Cliff: Employees in their first 2 years account for 42% of all organizational exits.
  • Overtime Exposure: Attrition among overtime staff reaches 30.5%—nearly triple the non-overtime rate (10.4%).
  • Primary Cost Center: Sales Executives alone account for $4.63M (41.1%) of replacement costs, pinpointing the highest-ROI retention opportunity.
